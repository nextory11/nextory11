## Visionary Type Definition

Visionary represents:

- The ability to imagine future possibilities.
- The ability to see beyond the current situation.
- The desire to create a future that does not yet exist.
- The ability to continue believing in possibilities during uncertainty.

## Difference Boundary

- Challenge: “Taking courage to step forward into the unknown.”
- Visionary: “Seeing and designing the future that does not yet exist.”

## Psychological Design Principles

Visionary questions reveal how a person imagines possibility, gives meaning to uncertainty, designs a future beyond the present, and continues believing in potential before it becomes visible.

## Question Design Philosophy

The approved questions reproduce small human experiences in which the user naturally considers an unseen future. They do not expose the target type, and every answer remains a natural human choice rather than a personality label.
